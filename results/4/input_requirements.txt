I want to build a web-based hospital management system. The system should allow patients and staff to register and login using their email and password. Patients should be able to book, update, and cancel appointments with doctors. Doctors should be able to view their schedules and manage patient records. The system should allow staff to maintain patient medical histories and prescriptions. The system should send appointment reminders via email or SMS. Admin users should be able to manage doctors, patients, and appointments. The system should generate daily and monthly reports of hospital activities. The system should support at least 300 concurrent users. All patient data must be securely stored and comply with data protection standards.
